# Quick Start Guide - Santa's Workshop

## How to Open the App

### Option 1: Double-Click (Easiest!)
1. Navigate to: `/home/user/coding-hub/projects/web/santas-workshop/`
2. Double-click `index.html`
3. It should open in your default web browser

### Option 2: From Browser
1. Open Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari
2. Press `Ctrl+O` (or `Cmd+O` on Mac) to open a file
3. Navigate to the folder and select `index.html`
4. Click Open

### Option 3: Drag and Drop
1. Open your web browser
2. Drag `index.html` into the browser window
3. The app will load!

## First Time Setup

When you first open the app:
1. **Allow microphone access** (for "Talk to Santa" feature)
   - Your browser will ask for permission
   - Click "Allow" to enable voice features
2. **Turn on your speakers** for the best experience
3. **Use a tablet or computer** - bigger screen is better for toddlers

## Using the App with Your Child

### Home Screen
- Big colorful buttons for each activity
- Kids can tap any button to start

### My Nice List Stars
- When your child does something good, click the matching button
- They'll earn a star and hear encouragement!
- Stars are saved automatically

### My Wish List
- Click category buttons to add wishes
- A grown-up can help type specific items
- Wishes are saved for Santa to see

### Talk to Santa
1. Click the big "Talk to Santa" button
2. Santa will say he's listening
3. Your child speaks into the microphone
4. Santa responds with encouragement!

**Tips:**
- Speak clearly and close to the microphone
- Works best in Chrome or Edge browsers
- If voice doesn't work, you'll still see messages

### Meet the Elves
- Click each elf to hear them talk
- Four different elves with fun jobs
- They'll tell you about what they do at the North Pole

### Find My Elf
- Use this each morning during December
- Click where you think the elf is hiding
- Get encouraging messages about being good

## Troubleshooting

### No Sound?
- Check your device volume
- Make sure speakers/headphones are connected
- Try refreshing the page

### Voice Not Working?
- Use Chrome or Edge browser (best support)
- Make sure you allowed microphone access
- Check microphone settings in your browser
- The app still works without voice - you'll see text messages

### Buttons Not Working?
- Refresh the page (F5 or Cmd+R)
- Make sure JavaScript is enabled in your browser

## Tips for Parents

- **Make it a routine**: Use daily throughout December
- **Supervise voice features**: Help younger children speak clearly
- **Celebrate stars**: Get excited when they earn stars for good deeds
- **Keep it positive**: The app focuses on encouragement, not punishment
- **Connect to real life**: When you move your physical Elf on the Shelf, use the tracker in the app

## Privacy

- All data stays on your device (no internet required)
- Stars and wishes saved in browser storage
- No information is sent anywhere
- Completely safe for children

## Enjoy!

Have a magical Christmas season with Santa's Workshop!
