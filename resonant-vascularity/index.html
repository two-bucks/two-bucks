Resonant Vascularity

A Generative Art Exploration of Organic Growth

About This Simulation

This piece explores organic growth through three phases:

  1. Phase 1: Growing the Soil - A reaction-diffusion simulation creates fertile patterns
  2. Phase 2: Sampling - The system reads the soil to understand where growth can occur
  3. Phase 3: Growing Vines - Organic structures emerge, guided by the fertile soil

Watch as the vines avoid barren areas and flourish in the green, fertile regions of the soil.
