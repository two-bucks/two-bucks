      🫂 Emotional Bids

      Any attempt to connect. The building blocks of trust.

      An emotional bid is any attempt to connect — a comment, a sigh, a touch, a look. Your partner makes dozens of bids daily. How you respond determines the trust bank account.

      🫂
      Turn Towards

      Respond genuinely. Engage with the bid. Ask a follow-up. Builds trust.

      😶
      Turn Away

      Ignore or give minimal response. Often unintentional. Slowly erodes trust.

      💥
      Turn Against

      Reject or criticize the bid. Hostile response. Damages trust quickly.
